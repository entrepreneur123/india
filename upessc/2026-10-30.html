    Chairman's Message

    After taking charge as the Chairman of the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission, my topmost priority is to ensure a fully transparent and merit-based selection process in line with the government's policies, with zero tolerance without any compromise on integrity. While advancing recruitments in a time-bound manner, we will further strengthen technology-based processes, so that every eligible candidate gets their rightful opportunity. My goal is not just appointments, but to guarantee the better future of youth’s and strengthen their trust. Best wishes to all for a bright future.

      ABOUT US

      The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission Act, 2023 was notified on August 21, 2023, through U.P. Act No. 15 of 2023. In accordance with Section 27 of this Act, the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission Rules, 2023 were published by the Government of Uttar Pradesh, Higher Education Department-5 via Notification No. 3118/Seventy-5-2023-25-2019-T.C.-18 dated December 13, 2023.According to Section 2(1)(d) of the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission Act, 2023, the term "Teacher" refers to any individual appointed for the purpose of imparting instruction in an institution, and includes Principals, Vice-Principals, and Head Teachers.Thus, under the newly constituted Commission, online requisitions (adhiyachans) will be received for the selection of: Principals/Headmasters/Head Teachers of the aforementioned departments, Assistant Professors in colleges, Lecturers (PGTs) in Intermediate Colleges and Atal Residential Schools, Assistant Teachers in High Schools, Junior High Schools, affiliated Primary Schools, Atal Residential Schools, and Instructors in Government ITIs.
