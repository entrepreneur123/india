Chairman's Message

Public Service is the greatest opportunity to serve the nation. When a public servant uses it for personal gain, they cause the greatest disservice to the nation. The individuals chosen by the GPSC are future leaders, administrators and change makers who will serve the people of Gujarat with dedication and integrity. Through their commitment and leadership, we will move forward towards achievement of development with inclusive socio-economic development in harmony with the nature.

Hasmukh Patel, IPS (Retd.)

- Chairman

About the Gujarat Public Service Commission

The Gujarat Public Service Commission (GPSC) is a constitutional body established to conduct examinations for appointments to the services of the State of Gujarat. We are committed to upholding the principles of merit, equity, and fairness in the recruitment process.
