Yaari Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. This policy explains what the Yaari app and website (yaari) collect, why, who processes it and how you can delete it. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Rules, 2021; people in other countries have the same rights, listed below.

Who can use Yaari

Yaari is only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly process children's data. Accounts that appear to belong to someone under 18 are removed.

What we collect and why

DataWhy

Name, gender, date of birth, city, bio, profile photoYour profile. Other people see your name, gender, age (not your date of birth), city, bio and photo only after they have been matched with you. Gender and date of birth cannot be changed after sign-up, to prevent fake profiles.
Interests and extra profile photos (optional)Up to 5 interest tags you choose and up to 4 more photos. They show on your profile to the same people who can see your profile. Video callsIn the app and on the website, the other person's video is checked for nudity on your own device; no picture leaves it. If nudity is detected, the video is hidden and a report (who, when, no images) goes to our safety team. Calls themselves are not recorded.
RoomsTo run rooms (talk, music and games): chat, live audio, shared music and Ludo. People in the same room see your name, age, city and photo, and while you are on the stage anyone browsing rooms can see them too. Your voice is relayed live only while someone is on the stage, and is not recorded. Room chat (including photos and question cards), raised hands, song votes, reactions and Ludo results are stored so everyone in the room sees them; the room's settings (who may join, its code, its announcement) are stored with the room.
Photos in roomsBefore a photo is sent to a room, it is checked for nudity on your own device; nothing leaves your device for this check, and a photo that looks like nudity is not sent. Photos are stored privately and shown only to people in that room (or there in the last day), blurred until they tap to view.
NotificationsYour in-app notifications (friend requests and acceptances, a friend's new messages, room invites) record who, what kind and when, never the message text. To send phone notifications, the app stores a notification token for your device with its language and time zone (so nothing is sent at night, 23:00 to 08:00 your time, except friend requests); a notification carries only the sender's name and the kind of event. We note when you last had the app open so no notification is sent while you are using it. Phone notifications are delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can turn them off in your phone's settings; signing out removes the token from use.
DiagnosticsTo find and fix crashes and see whether the main steps work (signing up, getting a match, sending a first message, opening or joining a room, starting Ludo or a video call, buying Plus). When something breaks we receive the error message and the place in the code where it happened, the app version, the platform (web or Android) and the screen, linked to your account if you are signed in. For the main steps we receive only the step's name and the time. On the website we also count visits (the country worked out from your connection, the device type and the page that sent you; the address itself is not kept) and the app language. Diagnostics never contain your messages, photos, phone number or what you say in calls, and they are never used for advertising. The Android app keeps them on our own server (Supabase); the website sends errors to Sentry and the steps and visits to PostHog (in the EU), which process them only for us.
Identity check (ID document and a live selfie)So that each person has one account and gender, age and country are real. The check runs in Didit's verification page: you photograph an identity document and take a short live selfie, which is compared with the document photo. We receive and keep only your gender, date of birth, nationality and issuing country, and a one-way fingerprint (salted hash) of the document number, used to stop the same document verifying another account; we do not keep your name, document number, document images or selfie. After the check, your gender, date of birth and country on Yaari come from the document and cannot be changed. People under 18 are not allowed. Others see only that you are verified.
Chat ratings (manners)After a random chat in which you both wrote, each of you can rate the chat as good or not good with up to three tags. Ratings from the last 90 days make a manners score; others see the score and the good tags you received, never who rated or any bad tags. A very low score pauses random chat.
Purchase records from Google PlayTo grant Premium or the 24-hour gender pass. We receive a purchase token from Google; we never see your card or UPI details.

Video calls and room audio are relayed live and are not recorded by Yaari. In the Android app, screenshots and screen recording are blocked in chats, video calls and other people's profiles; a website cannot block them.

On the website, your sign-in session, language choice and whether you have seen the welcome tour are kept in your browser's local storage. The website uses no advertising or analytics cookies and stores nothing in your browser for diagnostics.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in), hosted in the Mumbai, India region.
  • LiveKit Cloud (relaying video calls and room audio), through the server nearest to the people talking.
  • YouTube (the shared music player in rooms), loaded only when a room plays music and you open the player. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to what it plays.
  • Didit (identity check: document and live selfie, run on its own verification page; it sends us only the results listed above).
  • Sentry (website error reports) and PostHog, EU region (website usage diagnostics).
  • Google Play (payments, and refund notices that end what a refunded purchase gave), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering phone notifications), our SMS provider (sending sign-in codes) and Vercel (serving the website).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access rules in the database let people read only what they are allowed to see. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: Yaari's systems can access them to handle reports and legal requests.

How long we keep data

Your profile and posts: until you delete your account. Other data is removed automatically:

  • Random chats (messages and photos): 30 days after the chat ends. Chats with people you added as friends stay until one of you removes the other.
  • Room chat, including photos sent to rooms: 30 days. Rooms: 7 days after they end. The audio itself is never stored.
  • Old profile photos, photos of deleted chats or posts, and room photos past their 30 days: within a day or two.
  • Reports: one year, with a copy of the reported chat or room messages (what the reported person wrote or posted, photos included, in the last day) so we can review them, even if either account is deleted. The reported person does not see who reported them.
  • Notifications: 90 days after you have read them (180 days if never read). Notification tokens of devices no longer in use: 30 days.
  • Diagnostics (error reports and step names): 90 days. If you delete your account they stay for those 90 days without the link to your account.
  • If an account is deleted while it is suspended, we keep only a one-way hash of the phone number until the suspension ends, so that a suspension cannot be avoided by signing up again.
  • When an account is deleted, we keep a one-way hash of the phone number with the gender and date of birth for one year, so that signing up again with the same number cannot change them. It is then deleted automatically.

Your rights

  • Delete everything: in the app or on the website, Me → Settings → Delete account. Your profile, posts, chats, photos and videos are deleted permanently. See how to delete your account if you can't sign in.
  • Correct or access your data, or withdraw consent: edit your profile in the app or write to the Grievance Officer below.
  • You can also ask for a copy of your data. We answer requests within 30 days.
  • If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or to your own country's authority (for example the ANPD in Brazil).
