Yaari Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. This policy explains what the Yaari app and website (yaari) collect, why, who processes it and how you can delete it. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Rules, 2021; people in other countries have the same rights, listed below.

Who can use Yaari

Yaari is only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly process children's data. Accounts that appear to belong to someone under 18 are removed.

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Mobile numberSign-in by one-time code, and one account per number.
Name, gender, date of birth, city, bio, profile photoYour profile. Other people see your name, gender, age (not your date of birth), city, bio and photo only after they have been matched with you. Gender and date of birth cannot be changed after sign-up, to prevent fake profiles.
Posts, chat messages, photos and videos you sendTo deliver them to the people you choose. Media is stored privately and shown only to people you have been matched with.
Matches, reports and blocksTo run matching, keep blocked people away from you, and act on reports (including automatic suspension after repeated reports).
Purchase records from Google PlayTo grant Premium or the 24-hour gender pass. We receive a purchase token from Google; we never see your card or UPI details.

Video calls are relayed live between the two people and are not recorded by Yaari. In the Android app, screenshots and screen recording are blocked in chats, video calls and other people's profiles; a website cannot block them.

On the website, your sign-in session and language choice are kept in your browser's local storage. The website uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in), hosted in the Mumbai, India region.
  • LiveKit Cloud (relaying video calls), through the server nearest to the two callers.
  • Google Play (payments), our SMS provider (sending sign-in codes) and Vercel (serving the website).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access rules in the database let people read only what they are allowed to see. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: Yaari's systems can access them to handle reports and legal requests.

How long we keep data

Your profile and posts: until you delete your account. Other data is removed automatically:

  • Random chats (messages and photos): 30 days after the chat ends. Chats with people you added as friends stay until one of you removes the other.
  • Room messages: 30 days. Rooms people create: 7 days after they close.
  • Old profile photos and photos of deleted chats or posts: within a day or two.
  • Reports: one year, with a copy of the reported chat or room messages so we can review them, even if either account is deleted. The reported person does not see who reported them.
  • If an account is deleted while it is suspended, we keep only a one-way hash of the phone number until the suspension ends, so that a suspension cannot be avoided by signing up again.

Your rights

  • Delete everything: in the app or on the website, Me → Settings → Delete account. Your profile, posts, chats, photos and videos are deleted permanently. See how to delete your account if you can't sign in.
  • Correct or access your data, or withdraw consent: edit your profile in the app or write to the Grievance Officer below.
  • You can also ask for a copy of your data. We answer requests within 30 days.
  • If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or to your own country's authority (for example the ANPD in Brazil).
