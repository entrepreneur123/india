FROM THE DESK OF CHAIRMAN

Dr. Sandeep Mittal, IPS

Director General of Police and Chairman


"Every year, thousands of young men and women across our state dream of wearing the uniform, of standing for something larger than themselves, protecting their communities and building a career rooted in service and pride. The Tamil Nadu Uniformed Services Recruitment Board turns that dream in to a real fair opportunity.
Since 1991, Board has recruited the officers who go on to serve in the Police, Fire and Rescue, and Prison Departments of Tamil Nadu. We know that what this journey means to you the early mornings of preparation, the physical training, the long hours of study. That is why we hold ourselves to a process built on transparency and merit, so that your hard work is what decides your future, nothing else. I encourage every aspirant to stay connected with this website for authentic updates on our recruitment activities, eligibility, and examination schedules.
To the youth of Tamil Nadu preparing for this path, your ambition is the strength of tomorrow's uniformed services. Prepare with discipline, apply with confidence, and know that a genuine opportunity awaits you here".
