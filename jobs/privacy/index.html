Privacy policy: Sarkari Job Alerts

गोपनीयता नीति

Last updated 28 Oct 2026.

No account

There is no sign-up. We never ask for your name, phone number, e-mail or documents.

What stays on your phone

Your eligibility answers (date of birth, category, qualification), saved notices, followed exams, roll numbers you check, language and theme are kept in your browser's storage on this device only. They are not sent to us. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them.

Result check by roll number

The app downloads the part of the published result list that could contain your number (picked by its last two digits) and looks for the full number on your phone. The full number is not sent.

Alerts

Only if you turn on alerts: your browser's push service gives an address for your browser. We keep that address, its keys, the exam codes you follow, your language and when you last changed them on our server to send the alerts, and nothing else. Turning alerts off deletes them; if you are offline at that moment, they are deleted the next time an alert cannot be delivered.

Channels and links

Our WhatsApp and Telegram channels are run inside those apps, under their own privacy policies. Links to recruiters' websites take you to sites with their own policies.

Server logs, cookies, ads

Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages may keep technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security. This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising at present. If that changes, this page will say so first.

AboutPrivacy policyTerms of use

Sarkari Job Alerts is not a government website and is not linked to any recruiting body. Always check the official notice before you apply.
