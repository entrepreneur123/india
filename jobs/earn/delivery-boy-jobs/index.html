Delivery Boy Jobs 2026: Blinkit, Zomato, Swiggy, Zepto, Flipkart — How to Join (Official Apps)

डिलीवरी बॉय जॉब 2026: ब्लिंकिट, ज़ोमैटो, स्विगी, ज़ेप्टो — कैसे जॉइन करें

Food, grocery and parcel delivery is the most common way to start earning quickly in Indian cities. You join through each company's own partner app: it asks for your documents, checks them and shows the work near you. Earnings are paid per order or per delivery; the amounts below are what each company states on its official app page, not a fixed salary.

  • Blinkit — Grocery delivery. Earn up to ₹30,000 per month; Orders from a nearby store, within 3 km; Daily or weekly payouts; Joining bonus up to ₹6,000*; ₹10 lakh life insurance and ₹1 lakh health insurance; EV bike on rent. 1 crore+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • Zomato — Food delivery. Earn up to ₹30,000 per month, paid per delivery; Join with Aadhaar and PAN, then online training; Works in 500+ cities. 1 crore+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • Swiggy — Food delivery. Earn up to ₹60,000 per month; Joining bonus up to ₹10,000; Works in 600+ cities. 1 crore+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • Zepto — Grocery delivery. Paid per order, with a minimum business guarantee; Deliveries within 3 km of the store. 1 crore+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • Shadowfax — Parcel delivery. Earn up to ₹45,000+ per month, weekly payouts; Anyone 18+ with valid Aadhaar, PAN and a bank account; Bike, scooter, bicycle or EV (rent available) and an Android phone; Insurance up to ₹7.5 lakh; 2,500+ cities. 1 crore+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • Flipkart (Ekart) — Parcel delivery. Earn up to ₹40,000 per month, paid weekly or fortnightly; No investment required: Aadhaar, PAN card and a bike; Parcel, grocery and 10-minute delivery roles. 10 lakh+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • Delhivery — Parcel delivery. Earn up to ₹40,000 per month, weekly payouts; Needs Aadhaar, PAN, a bank account, an Android phone; age 18+; EV on rent at ₹150 a day; Eligible partners may move to on-roll employment. 10 lakh+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • BigBasket — Grocery delivery. 10 lakh+ downloads. Official app ↗

From each company's or ministry's official page, checked on 30 Oct 2026. "Up to" amounts are what the company says, not a promise.

How to join

  1. Install the official app from Google Play (link above). Do not install an APK sent by anyone.
  2. Register with your mobile number.
  3. Upload the documents the app asks for and finish its verification.
  4. Start work when the company activates your account.

How to spot a fake job or earning offer

  • They ask you to pay a registration, joining, kit or 'security' fee to an agent or a person before you get work
  • They pay a little for small 'tasks' (likes, reviews, typing) and then ask you to invest more
  • Contact only through WhatsApp or Telegram, never through an official app or website
  • Promises like ₹5,000–₹10,000 a day from home with no skills
  • An APK file to install from a link instead of Google Play

If you were cheated, report it on cybercrime.gov.in or call the cybercrime helpline 1930.
