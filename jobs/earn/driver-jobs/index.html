Driver Jobs 2026: Rapido, Uber, Ola, Namma Yatri, Porter — Bike, Auto, Cab and Tempo (Official Apps)

ड्राइवर जॉब 2026: रैपिडो, उबर, ओला, पोर्टर — बाइक, ऑटो, कैब

If you have a driving licence and a vehicle (bike, auto, cab or goods vehicle), you can earn by giving rides or moving goods through these apps. Each company checks your licence and vehicle papers in its own driver app. What you earn depends on the rides you take; the facts below are from each company's official app page.

  • Rapido — Bike, auto and cab rides. Bike taxi, auto and cab rides; ₹5 lakh* insurance on every ride. 1 crore+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • Uber — Bike, auto and cab rides. 10 crore+ (worldwide) downloads. Official app ↗
  • Ola — Bike, auto and cab rides. 1 crore+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • Namma Yatri — Bike, auto and cab rides. Zero commission: you keep 100% of the fare; Needs driving licence and vehicle registration (RC); Bengaluru, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kochi, Mysuru and Tumakuru. 10 lakh+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • Porter — Goods transport (bike to mini-truck). Bike, three-wheeler, tempo, Tata Ace, mini-truck and pickup; Instant or daily payouts; No forced login or shift commitments; 34+ cities. 1 crore+ downloads. Official app ↗

From each company's or ministry's official page, checked on 30 Oct 2026. "Up to" amounts are what the company says, not a promise.

How to join

  1. Install the official app from Google Play (link above). Do not install an APK sent by anyone.
  2. Register with your mobile number.
  3. Upload the documents the app asks for and finish its verification.
  4. Start work when the company activates your account.

How to spot a fake job or earning offer

  • They ask you to pay a registration, joining, kit or 'security' fee to an agent or a person before you get work
  • They pay a little for small 'tasks' (likes, reviews, typing) and then ask you to invest more
  • Contact only through WhatsApp or Telegram, never through an official app or website
  • Promises like ₹5,000–₹10,000 a day from home with no skills
  • An APK file to install from a link instead of Google Play

If you were cheated, report it on cybercrime.gov.in or call the cybercrime helpline 1930.
