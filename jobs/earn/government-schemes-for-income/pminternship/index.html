PM Internship Scheme 2026: Eligibility and How to Apply (Official)

Paid internships in top companies. You join through the official app PM Internship by Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India on Google Play (1 lakh+ downloads).

Open the official app ↗

What PM Internship Scheme says

  • For youth aged 18–25 who are not in full-time employment
  • Family income below ₹12 lakh a year
  • Apply for up to three internships with no fees

Quoted from the official page on 30 Oct 2026; "up to" amounts and bonuses are the company's own claims and can change.

How to join

  1. Open the official link above and follow its steps.

How to spot a fake job or earning offer

  • They ask you to pay a registration, joining, kit or 'security' fee to an agent or a person before you get work
  • They pay a little for small 'tasks' (likes, reviews, typing) and then ask you to invest more
  • Contact only through WhatsApp or Telegram, never through an official app or website
  • Promises like ₹5,000–₹10,000 a day from home with no skills
  • An APK file to install from a link instead of Google Play

If you were cheated, report it on cybercrime.gov.in or call the cybercrime helpline 1930.
