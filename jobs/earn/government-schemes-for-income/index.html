Government Schemes to Earn 2026: PM Internship, e-Shram, Skill India, Apprenticeship, Mudra Loan, PM SVANidhi

कमाई के लिए सरकारी योजनाएँ 2026: पीएम इंटर्नशिप, ई-श्रम, स्किल इंडिया, अप्रेंटिसशिप, मुद्रा लोन, पीएम स्वनिधि

Free government help for people who want work or want to start their own: internships with a stipend, skill courses, apprenticeships, a card for unorganised and gig workers, and small loans without collateral. Apply only on the official sites below; nobody needs to be paid to get you in.

  • PM Internship Scheme — Paid internships in top companies. For youth aged 18–25 who are not in full-time employment; Family income below ₹12 lakh a year; Apply for up to three internships with no fees. 1 lakh+ downloads. Official app ↗
  • e-Shram — Free card for unorganised and gig workers. Official site ↗
  • Skill India Digital — Skill courses and certificates. Official site ↗
  • Apprenticeship India — Apprenticeships with a monthly stipend. Official site ↗
  • PM Mudra Yojana — Loans to start your own work. Loans without collateral to start or grow a small business; Shishu up to ₹50,000; Kishore ₹50,000–₹5 lakh; Tarun ₹5–10 lakh; Tarun Plus up to ₹20 lakh for those who repaid a Tarun loan; Apply online on Udyam Mitra or at a bank. Official site ↗
  • PM SVANidhi — Loans for street vendors. Loans for street vendors: ₹15,000, then ₹25,000, then ₹50,000; Needs a Certificate of Vending or a Letter of Recommendation from the Urban Local Body. Official site ↗

From each company's or ministry's official page, checked on 30 Oct 2026. "Up to" amounts are what the company says, not a promise.

How to spot a fake job or earning offer

  • They ask you to pay a registration, joining, kit or 'security' fee to an agent or a person before you get work
  • They pay a little for small 'tasks' (likes, reviews, typing) and then ask you to invest more
  • Contact only through WhatsApp or Telegram, never through an official app or website
  • Promises like ₹5,000–₹10,000 a day from home with no skills
  • An APK file to install from a link instead of Google Play

If you were cheated, report it on cybercrime.gov.in or call the cybercrime helpline 1930.
