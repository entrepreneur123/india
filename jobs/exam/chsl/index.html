Application fee

₹100 for General / OBC / EWS. No fee for: women, SC, ST, persons with benchmark disabilities, ex-servicemen. Correction charges ₹200 / ₹500.

  • 18-27 years: born 2 Aug 1999 to 1 Aug 2008

Upper age relaxation: SC 5 years, ST 5 years, OBC 3 years, PwBD 10 years, PwBD OBC 13 years, PwBD SC 15 years, PwBD ST 15 years.

  • Level 4: basic pay ₹25,500 to ₹81,100 a month

Basic pay from the Notice; allowances are extra.
