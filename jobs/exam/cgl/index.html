Basic pay from the Notice; allowances are extra.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to apply for SSC CGL 2026?

25 Jun 2026. Fee payment closes on 26 Jun 2026.

What is the age limit for SSC CGL 2026?

18-27 years: born 2 Aug 1999 to 1 Aug 2008; 20-30 years: born 2 Aug 1996 to 1 Aug 2006; 18-30 years: born 2 Aug 1996 to 1 Aug 2008; 18-32 years: born 2 Aug 1994 to 1 Aug 2008 (as on 1 Aug 2026), plus category relaxation.

What is the SSC CGL application fee?

₹100; no fee for women, SC, ST, persons with benchmark disabilities, ex-servicemen.

What is the SSC CGL salary?

Level 4: ₹25,500 to ₹81,100 basic pay a month; Level 5: ₹29,200 to ₹92,300 basic pay a month; Level 6: ₹35,400 to ₹1,12,400 basic pay a month; Level 7: ₹44,900 to ₹1,42,400 basic pay a month; Level 8: ₹47,600 to ₹1,51,100 basic pay a month. Allowances are extra.

When is the SSC CGL 2026 exam?

Tier-I (CBE): 30 Sep 2026 to 30 Oct 2026 (tentative); Tier-II (CBE): December, 2026 (tentative)

What qualification is needed for SSC CGL?

Bachelor's degree.
