Information only

We collect notices from official websites and try to show them correctly and quickly, but we can make mistakes or be late. Before you apply, pay or travel, read the official notice: it always comes first.

Eligibility check and result search

These are aids worked out from the official notice and result lists. They are not official decisions; the recruiter decides who is eligible and who has passed.

We never ask for money

This site is free. We never ask for fees, bank details or documents, and we never promise a job. Anyone who asks for money to get you a government job is committing fraud: report it to the police or at cybercrime.gov.in.

Other websites

Notices and files belong to the recruiters that published them. We are not responsible for other websites' content or availability.
