About Sarkari Job Alerts

हमारे बारे में

Sarkari Job Alerts is a free website that puts the latest government job notices in India in one place: new recruitments, admit cards, answer keys, results and exam dates. It also checks your eligibility for SSC posts from the official notice and finds your roll number in published result lists. It works in English and 12 Indian languages.

Where the information comes from

Only from the recruiters' own websites:

  • Staff Selection Commission (SSC): ssc.gov.in
  • UP Police Recruitment & Promotion Board (UPPRPB): uppbpb.gov.in
  • Haryana Staff Selection Commission (HSSC): hssc.gov.in
  • Central Selection Board of Constable, Bihar (CSBC): csbc.bihar.gov.in
  • Bihar Police Sub-ordinate Service Commission (BPSSC): bpssc.bihar.gov.in
  • Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO): www.isro.gov.in
  • Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC): ongcindia.com

How it is kept up to date

A program reads these notice boards several times a day. Every update links to the recruiter's official file or page, and titles are shown as the recruiter published them.

Not a government website

We are not part of, or linked to, any government or recruiting body. If anything here differs from the official notice, the official notice is right.
