Common questions

When are the results declared?

Dear lottery draws are at 1 PM (Nagaland), 6 PM (Sikkim) and 8 PM (Nagaland) IST every day. The result sheet usually appears 7–10 minutes after each draw, and this page updates by itself.

How do I check my ticket?

Type the full number (e.g. 39C 06366, bumper A 801021) or just the last 4–6 digits.

How do I claim a prize?

Nagaland prizes above ₹10,000 must be claimed within 30 days of the draw at the Directorate of Nagaland State Lotteries, Kohima, or its nodal offices, with the original ticket and 3 attested passport photos (as printed on the official result). Ask your ticket seller about smaller prizes. Tax is deducted on large prizes.

For Sikkim and Punjab draws, follow the claim instructions printed on your ticket and on the official result. Ask your seller first.

Stay safe

No app, agent or WhatsApp group can predict or fix lottery numbers. LottoDost never sells tickets and never asks for money, bank or UPI details. Treat any "guaranteed number" offer as fraud.
